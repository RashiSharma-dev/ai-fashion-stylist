CHATBOT SCOPE & PLANNED Q&A — AI Fashion Stylist Chatbot
===========================================================
Chatbot type: API-based (Groq, Llama 3 model — free tier, fast)
Scope: Fashion color & styling advice only, personalized using
       the user's detected skin tone and uploaded outfit data.
Out of scope: Sizing/fit, shopping links, non-fashion topics.
===========================================================

1. Q: What colors suit a warm skin tone?
   A: Warm earthy tones like mustard, olive, terracotta, and
      warm reds work well because they complement warm undertones.

2. Q: What colors suit a cool skin tone?
   A: Jewel tones like emerald, sapphire blue, and true reds
      complement cool undertones better than warm earthy shades.

3. Q: What should I wear for a job interview?
   A: Navy blue, charcoal grey, or black convey professionalism
      and confidence. Avoid overly bright or busy patterns.

4. Q: What are good summer colors?
   A: Light pastels, whites, and soft blues feel fresh and
      reflect heat rather than absorb it.

5. Q: What are good winter colors?
   A: Deep jewel tones, burgundy, forest green, and darker
      neutrals suit the winter mood and colder palette.

6. Q: What colors go well with a pink shirt?
   A: Navy, white, grey, and soft denim blue pair well with
      pink without clashing.

7. Q: I have a neutral skin tone, what colors suit me best?
   A: Neutral skin tones are flexible — both warm (coral, peach)
      and cool (lavender, teal) shades can work well.

8. Q: What should I wear to a wedding as a guest?
   A: Avoid white (reserved for the bride). Soft pastels, jewel
      tones, or elegant neutrals are safe, stylish choices.

9. Q: Can I mix warm and cool colors in one outfit?
   A: It's possible but riskier — usually one neutral piece
      (white, black, grey, beige) helps bridge warm and cool tones.

10. Q: What colors should I avoid with a cool skin tone?
    A: Very warm, muted earthy tones like mustard or burnt
       orange can wash out cool undertones.

11. Q: What's a good color combination for a casual outfit?
    A: Denim blue with white, or beige with olive green, are
       easy, low-risk casual combinations.

12. Q: What colors work well for a party outfit?
    A: Bold jewel tones, metallics, or deep reds/blacks tend to
       stand out well in evening/party settings.

14. Q: What's the difference between analogous and complementary colors?
    A: Analogous colors sit next to each other on the color wheel
       (e.g., blue-green-teal) and blend smoothly. Complementary
       colors sit opposite each other (e.g., blue-orange) and
       create bold contrast.

15. Q: I'm not sure what colors suit me — where do I start?
